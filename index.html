Privacy policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Android package: com.highor.higypt

This policy explains how Highor handles information in connection with Higypt. For privacy questions, contact support@highor.com.

Higypt does not require a game account. The game has no advertising, analytics service, player tracking, or in-app purchases. Your game progress and journal notes are stored on your device and are not sent to Highor by the game.

Information stored on your device

Higypt stores your game progress, player position, inventory, puzzle choices, save dates, save thumbnails, graphics and control settings, and notes you enter in the game journal locally. Save thumbnails show the game world; they are not photographs from your device camera.

Highor does not receive these save files or journal notes automatically. Device or operating-system backup services may handle app data according to your device settings and their own policies.

Collection, sharing and permissions

Higypt does not collect or share personal data through gameplay. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. It has no chat, public journal, social feed or player-to-player communication. The game does not sell personal information.

The Android package includes engine and Google Play components and permissions for functions such as app distribution, licensing, network status, audio and keeping the screen awake. These permissions do not mean that Higypt sends your journal or progress to Highor.

Google Play

Google Play handles app installation, updates and any purchase of the app. Google may process account, payment, device and diagnostic information under its own policies. Highor does not receive your complete payment-card details through Higypt. Google Play may make order information, aggregated statistics and crash or performance reports available to the developer for app management and troubleshooting.

See Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's services.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate problems. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed for the request and applicable record-keeping obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence by contacting the address below, subject to those obligations.

Deleting your data

You can remove locally stored game data using Android Settings > Apps > Higypt > Storage > Clear storage, or by uninstalling the game. This deletes progress and notes from the app on that device. Check your device's backup settings separately. Higypt has no game account to delete.

This website

This information page is hosted by GitHub Pages. Highor has added no analytics, advertising cookies or tracking scripts to this page. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes

We will update this policy if Higypt's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
